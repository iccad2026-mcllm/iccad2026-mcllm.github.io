General Information

State-of-the-art learning algorithms for deep neural networks (DNNs) impose significant challenges for hardware implementations, particularly on resource-constrained edge devices. This workshop brings together researchers and practitioners across architecture, algorithms, devices, and applications to discuss efficient on-chip learning and inference.

The workshop addresses the full stack — from emerging nanoelectronic devices to neuromorphic architectures and energy-efficient learning algorithms — and their deployment on intelligent mobile and edge platforms.

Key Topics

  • Synaptic plasticity and neuron motifs of learning dynamics
  • Computational models of cortical activities
  • Sparse learning, feature extraction and personalization
  • Deep learning with high speed and high power efficiency
  • Hardware acceleration for machine learning
  • Hardware emulation of brain
  • Nanoelectronic devices and architectures for neuro-computing
  • Applications of learning on a smart mobile platform
